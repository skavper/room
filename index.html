Notifications

Your phone tells you when a message comes. It also works when this page is closed. You need the free app "ntfy".

When you leave this page, it signs you out. That is normal. So do the steps in this order.

  1. Tap "Turn on".

    It is off.

  2. Copy your code.
  3. Get the app "ntfy" on your phone. ntfy for Android. ntfy for iPhone.
  4. Open the app. Tap the plus sign (+). Paste the code where it says "Topic name". Tap Subscribe. Or tap here. The app opens with the code.
  5. Come back to this page. Go in again. Then tap "Send a test". Your phone shows a notification.

  • The notification says "Room: New message". It does not show the message, or who wrote it. Open the room to read it.
  • The message itself comes when you are both in the room.
  • You can turn it off at any time. Tap "Turn off". Then nothing is sent to your phone.
  • The code belongs to your name. Type the same name every time.
  • This uses the free service ntfy.sh. It can see that a message was sent, and when. It can never see what the message says.
